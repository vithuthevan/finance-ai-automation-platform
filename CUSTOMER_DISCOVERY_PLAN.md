# Customer discovery plan

**Goal:** Learn what must be true for firms to **change behaviour and pay** — not to collect compliments.  
**Target:** **10 interviews** (minimum); expand to **20** if moderate+ signals on hypotheses 1–5 in `FOUNDER_PRODUCT_HYPOTHESES.md`.  
**Code/build:** Out of scope for this plan.

---

## 1. Segment respondents

| Segment | Quota (of first 10) | Why |
|---------|---------------------|-----|
| Firm owner / partner | 4 | Economic buyer |
| Practice manager / ops lead | 3 | Workflow owner |
| Bookkeeper / senior accountant | 3 | Daily user; can block |

**Firm profile (prioritize):** 2–15 staff, 15–60 SME clients, English-capable, mix of Xero/QBO and spreadsheet clients.  
**Sri Lanka first** where possible; 2–3 interviews from UK/AU/SG only if local pool thin — mark comparability.

**Recruiting:** Personal network, CA/lodge referrals, LinkedIn — no product demo in recruiting message.

---

## 2. Interview structure (45–60 min)

| Phase | Minutes | Rule |
|-------|---------|------|
| **FIRST — Current behaviour** | 20 | No product |
| **SECOND — Pain** | 10 | Still no product |
| **THIRD — Workarounds & tools** | 10 | List what they pay for |
| **FOURTH — Economic consequence** | 5 | Time, money, risk |
| **FIFTH — Concept / demo** | 10–15 | Only targeted workflow |
| **Close** | 5 | Pilot interest, intros |

**Never lead with:** “Would you buy my software?”

---

## 3. Core question bank (behaviour-first)

### Month-end & readiness

1. Walk me through your **last month-end**, client by client — what happened Monday vs day 10?
2. What **delayed** close last month? Top three causes?
3. How did you know **every client was complete**? Can you show the spreadsheet/checklist?
4. Tell me about the **last client that missed** close — what happened after?
5. How do **partners** see work across all clients today? How often do they ask accountants for status?
6. How do you know an accountant is **overloaded**?

### Documents & chase

7. Where do **missing documents** usually arrive? (WhatsApp, email, portal, in person)
8. What did you **chase clients for** last month? How many times per client?
9. Who does the chasing — same person who books?
10. What happens to a receipt sent on **WhatsApp** — step by step until it’s in the ledger?

### Systems & duplication

11. What is your **financial system of record** per client? Any exceptions?
12. What still happens **outside** that system?
13. What work gets **duplicated** between tools?
14. What software do you **pay for** today? What would be painful to replace?
15. If we asked you to enter expenses in a **second system** as well as Xero — what’s your reaction? How many lines/month is tolerable?

### Value & payment (late only)

16. What do you **hate doing** that feels like it shouldn’t need a qualified accountant?
17. If you saved **5 hours/month** on coordination, where would that go? Would that change hiring?
18. Have you tried **Karbon / Dext / Financial Cents / Jetpack**? What worked or didn’t?
19. What would make you add a **new** tool to the stack?
20. *(After demo, if earned)* Would you run a **paid pilot** on 1–3 clients? What would success look like?

## 4. Demo protocol (only if earned)

Show **one** path matched to their answer:

| They said… | Show… |
|------------|--------|
| Chase is the pain | Document request → owner upload → complete → blocker cleared |
| Partner visibility | Work queue portfolio: ready vs blocked with counts |
| CSV / no feeds | Bank import → match → close blocked until clear |
| Xero-only books | **Do not** show full ledger first — discuss OPTION B honestly |

**Aha moment hypothesis to test:**  
*“Here are my clients — I immediately see who can close and what blocks the rest.”*  
**Measure:** Do they lean in, ask “can I filter by accountant?”, or say “my sheet already does this”?

## 5. Signal framework

| Level | Example utterance | Weight |
|-------|-------------------|--------|
| VERY WEAK | “Looks nice.” | Ignore |
| WEAK | “I could see people using it.” | Ignore |
| MODERATE | “We have this problem.” | Log; need repetition |
| STRONG | “Can you solve X?” (specific) | Validate X |
| VERY STRONG | “Can we test with some clients?” | Pilot track |
| COMMERCIAL | “How much?” | Pricing discovery |
| STRONGEST | Agrees to **defined paid pilot** | Execute pilot runbook |

**Behaviour > compliments:** Show checklist, count chase messages, introduce colleague, share real (redacted) tracker.

## 8. Pilot hypothesis (post-discovery)

**Offer (example structure — validate pricing in interviews):**

- 1 firm, **3 clients**, **90 days**, founder-supported  
- **Written SOR** per client (platform vs Xero)  
- Success metrics (measure, don’t guess targets in advance):  
  - Count of chase messages / emails for pilot clients vs control month  
  - Days from period-end to “partner signed off”  
  - % open document requests completed via portal  
  - Partner weekly portfolio views (analytics if available; else self-report)  
  - Accountant hours logged on coordination (timesheet sample)

**Paid pilot fee:** COMMERCIAL SIGNAL — amount set after interview #8–10.

---

## 9. Pricing / value-metric discovery questions

- Do you prefer pricing per **firm**, per **client**, or per **user**? Why?
- What tools are priced per client today?
- What would make pricing feel **unfair**?

**Hypothesis to test:** Base firm fee + active clients under close management.

---

## 10. Top questions that could kill strategy (ask explicitly)

1. Would you enter transactions twice for visibility? (**Kill if universal no**)
2. Will SME owners use a login portal? (**Kill request wedge if no**)
3. Does Karbon/Dext already solve this for you? (**Kill if yes + satisfied**)
4. Is “ready to close” in software useful if GL is in Xero? (**Kill OPTION B**)
5. What budget line does this come from — or is there no budget? (**Kill if none**)

---

## 11. Positive signals to hunt

- Unprompted mention of close spreadsheet pain  
- Partner joins interview; asks about multi-client view  
- Shows existing tracker willingly  
- “We failed close because of missing docs” story with numbers  
- Asks for colleague on call  
- Offers anonymized sample checklist  
- Asks pilot timeline without being sold  
- Mentions paying for worse tools today
